Everything you need to reach your PC

Built in the spirit of Chrome Remote Desktop, made for a phone screen.

A pointer you can really use

A big on-screen mouse — up to 160 px, in three colours — that you grab and drag with your finger. Tap, double-tap, long-press to drag, two fingers to scroll, pinch to zoom.

Remote Support

Help family and friends: they generate a 12-digit code, you type it, they click Allow. No account needed on their PC, and they can stop sharing at any time.

Control your phone from your PC New

Click to tap, drag to swipe, type with your keyboard, Back / Home / Recents, phone sound, clipboard and files — in a window on your PC.

Private by design

Screen, sound, input and files travel directly between your devices, encrypted (WebRTC DTLS-SRTP). Your PIN is checked on your PC and never leaves it.

Sound, files and clipboard

Hear your PC, copy and paste both ways, and send files up to 1 GB in either direction — straight from device to device.

Curtain mode

Blank the PC's monitor while you work on it remotely, so people in the room can't see what you're doing.

Quick actions

Lock, restart, shut down, change the volume, take a screenshot or a webcam photo — without opening a session.

Sharp on any screen

Pick the stream resolution — Auto, presets from 640×360 up to 4K, or your own size. Your PC's display never changes.

Who connected, and when

Sign in on your PC to see a history of every connection: the account, the phone, the time and how long it lasted.

Drag the mouse with your finger

The pointer is as big as you want it, so you always know where you're clicking.

  • Grab the pointer and move it, or slide anywhere like a trackpad.
  • Long-press then drag to move windows and select text.
  • Pinch to zoom up to 4× — the view follows the pointer.
  • Turn your phone sideways and the PC screen fills it.

Your phone, on your PC

Reply to messages and use apps with your real keyboard and mouse.

  • Turn on “Available to my PCs” once; your PC lists the phone.
  • The phone asks you to allow every session.
  • Back, Home, Recents and notifications from the toolbar.
  • Hear the phone's sound and move files both ways.

Up and running in three steps

One account connects your phone and your PCs.

Install both apps

Remote PC on your Android phone, Remote PC Host on your Windows PC. Create your account in either.

Set up the PC

Sign in to Remote PC Host — or type the 6-digit code from the app — and choose a PIN.

Connect

Tap your PC in the app, enter your PIN, and you're in. Your PC can now also open your phone.

Questions

The short answers.

Is it free?

Yes. Both apps are free to download and use.

Can someone else see my screen?

Only the account that set up your PC can connect, and only with your PIN. For Remote Support, the person at the PC has to click Allow every time and sees a “Stop sharing” bar. Screen and input go directly between the devices, encrypted; they don't pass through our servers.

Do I need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

No. It works over the internet as long as both devices are online. On some very strict networks a direct connection may not be possible.

Can my PC control my phone when nobody is holding it?

No. Android requires someone at the phone to accept screen sharing for every session. That's a deliberate Android safety rule.

Why does Windows warn me when I open the app?

Remote PC Host isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognise it. Choose More info → Run anyway.

Is there an iPhone or Mac version?

Not yet. A web version to connect from any browser is on the roadmap.
